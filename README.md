# FinSight-AI-Multi-Asset-Quantitative-Intelligence-Backtesting-Platform

### Multi-Asset Quantitative Intelligence & Backtesting Platform

**FinSight AI** is a quantitative financial intelligence platform that analyzes **NVIDIA, Bitcoin, and Gold** using historical market data, financial indicators, risk metrics, strategy backtesting, market-regime analysis, and AI-powered explanations.

> **From Market Data → Quantitative Analysis → Risk → Backtesting → Intelligence → AI Explanation**

---

## 🎯 Problem

Financial platforms often show charts, indicators, and returns separately.

A number such as:

> "The asset returned 30%"

doesn't explain:

- How risky was the journey?
- How large was the worst decline?
- What were the indicators showing?
- Did a strategy actually outperform a benchmark?
- Under what historical market conditions did the strategy perform differently?
- What does all of this mean in simple terms?

**FinSight AI connects these layers into one analytical system.**

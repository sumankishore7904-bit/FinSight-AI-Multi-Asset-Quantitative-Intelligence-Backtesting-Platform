# FinSight AI — Judge Q&A

## 1. What problem are you solving?

Financial data is available everywhere, but raw prices and charts do not automatically explain return, risk, drawdown, correlations or strategy behavior.

FinSight AI combines quantitative analysis, risk analysis, backtesting and AI explanation into one platform.

---

## 2. Why NVIDIA, Bitcoin and Gold?

They represent different asset classes.

NVIDIA:
Technology equity.

Bitcoin:
Cryptocurrency.

Gold:
Precious-metal asset.

Using different asset types allows us to demonstrate differences in return, volatility, drawdown and correlation.

---

## 3. What makes your project different from a normal stock dashboard?

A normal dashboard may primarily display prices and charts.

FinSight AI combines:

- Financial calculations
- Risk analysis
- Technical indicators
- Multi-asset comparison
- Correlation
- Backtesting
- Quantitative intelligence
- AI explanation

The goal is to connect the numbers with understandable explanations.
